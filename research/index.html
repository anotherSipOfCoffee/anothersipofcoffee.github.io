Ongoing studies in modular timber construction and computational design.

01 · Timber kit-of-parts · In development

Modular building systems

Exploring how a limited set of reusable timber parts can support different building sizes and layouts. The work connects module grids, openings, and assembly logic with a parametric model.

Current questions focus on reducing unique parts, coordinating connections, and translating geometry into drawings and part schedules. Engineering review and physical testing remain part of the development process.
