Timber Kit-of-Parts · 2026

Modular Housing Configurator

Not launched — in development

A modular building system made from a small kit of CNC-cut timber parts — posts, beams, joists, and swappable wall, floor, and roof panels — that combine on a simple grid. Change the size or layout and the whole design regenerates automatically: drawings, cut list, and part labels included.

The grid works at two scales: a fine 600 mm grid for windows, doors, and room layout, and a coarser 1,200 mm grid for the main frame. That combination keeps a small number of part types able to produce many genuinely different buildings, rather than one design repeated with different cladding.

This is still early-stage R&D — loosely benchmarked against kit-timber systems like Ludwig, but developed independently, with its own grid, parts, and connections. No structural claims yet: every connection and part goes through engineering review and physical testing before anything gets built for people to actually live in.
