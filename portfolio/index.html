concept · 2025

Jochy Tower

Jochy Tower is a mixed-use concept at the meeting point of the city and the open steppe. Offices, a hotel, housing, and commercial spaces are brought together in a tower whose terraces and planting draw on the movement of wind and water.

A canal links the tower to squares, promenades, and green spaces, with traffic kept at the perimeter. A landscaped podium connects the building to this public network; a mosque and the Seven Heavenly Gardens form a quiet crown above.

competition · 2025 · Al Khobar, Saudi Arabia

Eternal Pearls

Eternal Pearls is an urban sculpture proposal for Al Khobar, inspired by the Arabian Gulf’s pearl-diving heritage. A wave-like landscape holds three pearls of increasing size, forming a sequence from the shoreline toward the city.

The pearls represent past, present, and future: maritime roots, contemporary growth, and future ambition. Curved depressions and elevated pathways bring visitors into the composition, allowing the sculpture to be experienced through movement and changing viewpoints.

concept · 2025

Semey Mall

Semey Mall is an adaptive-reuse concept combining a retained brick industrial shell with new glass-and-steel volumes. Retail, leisure, and business spaces are arranged around courtyards and pedestrian routes, connecting the buildings with a shared public realm.

Transparent façades and clearly defined entrances guide visitors through the complex. Service and vehicle routes remain at the perimeter, while a flexible planning framework allows internal spaces to adapt to changing uses over time.

concept · 2024 · Vilnius, Lithuania

Urban Block

Urban Block is an academic proposal to transform a shopping-centre site between Šnipiškės and Šeškinė in Vilnius into a mixed-use quarter. Housing, workplaces, and civic uses surround green courtyards and pedestrian routes.

Perimeter buildings shelter quieter residential spaces from traffic, while an X-shaped pedestrian network connects the courtyards to a central gathering space. Underground parking frees the ground for public use; planted façades, rainwater collection, and solar installations extend the environmental strategy.
