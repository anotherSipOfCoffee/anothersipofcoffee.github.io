Mixed-Use Quarter — Urban Block

Vilnius, Lithuania

Program
Mixed-use quarter — faculty building, coworking, theatre, offices, hotel, apartments

Urban Block is an academic proposal to transform a shopping-centre site between Šnipiškės and Šeškinė in Vilnius into a mixed-use quarter. Housing, workplaces, and civic uses surround green courtyards and pedestrian routes.

Perimeter buildings shelter quieter residential spaces from traffic, while an X-shaped pedestrian network connects the courtyards to a central gathering space. Underground parking frees the ground for public use; planted façades, rainwater collection, and solar installations extend the environmental strategy.
