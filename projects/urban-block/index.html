Mixed-Use Quarter — Urban Block

Vilnius, Lithuania

Program
Mixed-use quarter — faculty building, coworking, theatre, offices, hotel, apartments

Urban Block is an academic proposal reimagining a shopping-center site at the intersection of two Vilnius districts as a sustainable, mixed-use urban quarter — combining housing, offices, and civic program around a system of green courtyards and pedestrian routes.

The proposed site sits at the intersection of two Vilnius districts — Šnipiškės and Šeškinė — currently occupied by a shopping center and a large surface parking lot. The redevelopment envisions a mixed-use urban quarter that responds to the needs of local residents while serving as a statement of contemporary sustainability principles, extending beyond design and material choices to encourage more sustainable lifestyles and habits.

To protect the site from noise and air pollution, perimeter buildings are proposed along the busy surrounding streets, with heights gradually increasing from the intersection in response to the urban context. Main buildings accommodate public, office, and commercial functions, while residential buildings sit within the quieter inner part of the quarter. Underground parking frees up more space for public areas.

The site is adjacent to the Neris Oxbow Park, the Japanese Garden, and the Šeškinė Slopes Nature Reserve — so natural elements are integrated throughout the development and into the building façades, including green façade systems, rainwater collection, and solar energy installations.

Site plan. The layout clearly separates circulation flows while creating a protected residential environment shielded from heavy traffic. Building groups are arranged in perimeter and semi-perimeter formations, defining a series of communal inner courtyards. An X-shaped pedestrian pathway system draws residents toward a central open space — the heart of everyday community life — while vehicle circulation and parking stay concentrated along the site’s perimeter.

The project aims to create a vibrant, sustainable, multifunctional environment that becomes a new destination between the Šnipiškės and Šeškinė districts.
