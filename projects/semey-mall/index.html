Retail & Mixed-Use — Semey Mall

Program
Retail & mixed-use — supermarket, boutiques, cinema, business center, gym
next

Semey Mall is an adaptive-reuse retail and leisure complex that threads new glass-and-steel volumes through a preserved brick industrial shell, bringing shopping, dining, and public space together around a network of courtyards and pedestrian routes.

The architectural expression is based on a simple and rational volumetric composition. The arrangement of the building volumes helps define public spaces, pedestrian connections, and visual corridors, strengthening the overall identity and legibility of the complex.

The facades incorporate transparent elements that create a strong visual connection between interior and exterior spaces, enhancing openness and interaction with the surrounding environment. Main entrances are clearly emphasized as key points of orientation for both pedestrians and arriving vehicles — architecture used not as decoration, but as a functional tool that communicates the building’s structure and guides movement.

Site organization. The site is organized around a clear functional zoning strategy and separation of circulation flows. Primary visitor circulation is concentrated around clearly defined entrances and public spaces, from which movement naturally extends throughout the complex without unnecessary repetition or dead ends — a continuous, intuitive flow that matters for the commercial success of the development. Transportation, service, and operational functions are organized separately along the site’s perimeter, minimizing their impact on public space.

The building’s spatial and planning framework is designed with flexibility in mind, so spaces can adapt to changing needs over time — keeping the development relevant and functional in the long term.
