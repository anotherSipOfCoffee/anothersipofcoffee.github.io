Retail & Mixed-Use — Semey Mall

Program
Retail & mixed-use — supermarket, boutiques, cinema, business center, gym

Semey Mall is an adaptive-reuse concept combining a retained brick industrial shell with new glass-and-steel volumes. Retail, leisure, and business spaces are arranged around courtyards and pedestrian routes, connecting the buildings with a shared public realm.

Transparent façades and clearly defined entrances guide visitors through the complex. Service and vehicle routes remain at the perimeter, while a flexible planning framework allows internal spaces to adapt to changing uses over time.

Programme areas
