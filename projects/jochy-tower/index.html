High-Rise Tower — Jochy Tower

Program
Mixed-use — commercial, offices, hotel, residential, mosque & sky gardens
next

Jochy Tower is envisioned as a new-generation urban landmark — an architectural statement that simultaneously represents technological progress, cultural identity, and the human connection to nature. The project is not conceived simply as a skyscraper, but as an urban system that shapes a new city center, a network of public spaces, and a new relationship between people, the city, and the landscape.

The tower sits at the point where the dense urban fabric transitions into the open horizon of the steppe. This threshold becomes the project’s central theme, with the architecture extending it vertically — its form, terraces, and greenery interpreting the movement of the steppe winds and the flow of the river.

Master plan. The site is organized not around a single building, but around a system of public spaces at the heart of the development. A central water axis serves as the main landmark and unifying element, with squares, green spaces, promenades, and pedestrian routes arranged around a canal that links the tower to the surrounding urban fabric. Fast-moving transportation runs along the perimeter, slower movement concentrates within public spaces, and a network of green areas connects architecture and landscape.

Architectural concept — podium, tower, crown. The podium extends the tower into fluid, organic spaces that connect nature, city, and people, with landscaped terraces and interconnected pathways. The glass-clad tower above brings together offices, a hotel, and residential space, with panoramic views opening from the upper levels. At the summit, a mosque and the “Seven Heavenly Gardens” form a place of prayer, reflection, and tranquility — their design drawing on geometry and nature to create a calm counterpoint to the tower’s rational structure below.

Scale.

  • Tower area: ~148,600 m²
  • Podium area: ~64,900 m²
  • Offices: ~80,900 m² · Commercial: ~64,900 m² · Hotel: ~19,800 m² · Residential: ~22,750 m²
