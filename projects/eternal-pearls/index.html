Urban Sculpture — Eternal Pearls

Al Khobar, Saudi Arabia

Program
Urban sculpture — proposal for the Buildner Urban Sculpture Competition
next

A wave rises from the sea, flowing across the shoreline and shaping the landscape. Its fluid form bends and undulates, creating elliptical depressions that cradle three pearls while also forming elevated pathways for visitors to explore and experience the surroundings. Within this wave, the pearls appear in different sizes, telling a story of growth, continuity, and transformation.

The smallest pearl, positioned closest to the sea, represents the past — the beginning of the journey, honoring the nation’s roots and its pearl-diving heritage. The middle pearl flourishes within the wave, embodying the present: prosperity, resilience, and growth amid challenges, much like an oasis thriving in the desert. The largest pearl, oriented toward the city, rises toward the sky and symbolizes the future — ambition, innovation, and limitless potential.

Together, the wave and the pearls create a symbolic journey from sea to city, from past to future, inviting visitors to experience the sculpture from multiple perspectives and discover new meanings along the way.

Context. Along the eastern coast of Al Khobar, on the shores of the Arabian Gulf, life was once deeply connected to the sea. Long before the discovery of oil, generations of pearl divers ventured into its waters in search of treasures hidden within oyster shells — gems that demanded courage, patience, and perseverance to find. These pearls symbolized survival, heritage, and the early aspirations of the nation, shaping not only the economy but the culture and identity of coastal communities. It is from this rich history that the inspiration for the sculpture emerges.
